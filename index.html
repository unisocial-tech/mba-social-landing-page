About MBA Social

MBA Social was founded in 2019 as a platform dedicated to institutional accountability, placement data integrity, and campus culture across India's top B-schools. Over the years, it has grown into one of India's largest MBA-focused communities, with a presence built on transparent reporting, community engagement, and student-first journalism.
